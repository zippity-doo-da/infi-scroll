Put source PNGs here. Minimum silhouette pack:

- One opaque sky band
- One transparent seamless city band
- One street/ground band
- Eight unique architecture sprites
- Four people at the canonical 100px world height
- Two traffic vehicles or one vehicle with directional variants
- Street props
- Optional low-frame-rate ambient animation strip

The installer trims transparent sprites, normalizes height, contracts light fringes, prepares horizontal seams, and packs animation cells according to `pack.json`.
