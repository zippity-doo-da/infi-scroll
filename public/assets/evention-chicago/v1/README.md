# Evention Chicago v1

A fictional, downtown-Chicago side-scrolling world. It does not represent Evention's real address.

## Visual rules

- Orthographic view across the Chicago River; never a perspective view down the river.
- Dense Loop canyon: limestone, terra cotta, Art Deco and dark glass towers.
- Thin skyline, Wacker/riverfront and foreground-river bands.
- Blue-hour navy, steel and warm-window palette with restrained Evention green.
- Customer names appear only inside architectural billboard frames on a separate transparent layer.
- Evention uses the supplied official logo artwork, never an improvised wordmark.
- No baked people, trains, boats or road traffic.
- One water taxi and one CTA train maximum on screen in their respective routes.
- The CTA pair uses a two-car 5000-series-style train with the route running above the riverfront.

## Included customer signage

Evention, Chipotle, Trader Joe's, Marriott and Hyatt.
